# The Rise of Autonomous Agents

Artificial Intelligence is moving beyond simple chat interfaces. The next frontier is **Agentic AI**—systems capable of reasoning, planning, and executing complex tasks.

## What defines an AI Agent?

1.  **Perception**: Understanding the environment.
2.  **Reasoning**: Planning a sequence of actions.
3.  **Action**: Executing code, calling APIs, or manipulating files.

```python
def create_agent(role):
    return Agent(
        role=role,
        goal="Automate boring tasks",
        tools=["Browser", "Terminal", "FileEditor"]
    )
```

As we integrate these agents into DevOps workflows, we see a massive shift in productivity.
